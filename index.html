I am a researcher specializing in Statistical Machine Learning, Statistical Inference, Computational Statistics, Explainable AI (XAI), and Data Science. I hold a Ph.D. in Statistics and an M.S. in Computer Science, with an interdisciplinary background spanning statistics, computer science, data science, and engineering. My research focuses on developing rigorous, interpretable, and data-driven methodologies for complex real-world problems, with particular interests at the intersection of Tourism Economics, Behavioral Science, and Pricing Science. I am especially interested in using statistical and AI-driven methods to understand human behavior and address classical questions in economics and the social sciences. By integrating statistical theory, machine learning, and explainable AI with human-centered applications, I aim to produce research that is both scientifically rigorous and practically meaningful.
